# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Media Gen to be safe, respectful, and constructive for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and help others participate.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's information without
  permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
personal attacks, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a channel listed on the repository
owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident.
Include relevant links or screenshots, but do not collect or share more
personal information than necessary.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban a
participant based on the severity and pattern of behavior. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical, and retaliation against a reporter is not
acceptable.
